Resume notes for the extremal-solution reproduction of arXiv:2303.12471
(work/kkr-extremal). Plain text; update after every milestone.

GOAL
  Reproduce the equal-spin asymptotically flat results of arXiv:2303.12471
  (fig. 1 left panels: a_H, s, t_H vs x and vs j), build a solver for the
  EXTREMAL solutions themselves, and obtain M_ext(J, alpha), mu(y) and the
  slope dM_ext/dalpha|_J directly at T = 0, to compare with the manuscript's
  T -> 0 extrapolation (results/egb-extremality.json).

DEAD ENDS (keep for the write-up)
  extremal_equations.py / extremal_solver.py : gauge g = r^2 with double-zero
     factored (b=(1-z^2)^2 B ...). Collocation near singular (cond 1e12-13,
     sigma_min -> 0 with N): near-extremal neighbours (B,F ~ 1/(x+delta)) are
     almost representable by polynomials. Abandoned.
  kkr_solver.py (xi = r^2/(1+r^2), power 1): KKR gauge b f = fixed works,
     but (i) at fixed (a, alpha) a smooth zero mode (physical size vs a)
     -> fix g_H = P2(0) = 1 ; (ii) residual gauge r -> r + c sqrt(b f) gives
     r^3 = xi^(3/2) at the horizon -> slow algebraic convergence in xi.
     The constraint-row replacement was WRONG (made things worse).
