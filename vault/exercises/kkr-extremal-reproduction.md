## Attempt (what worked, what did not)

1. Field equations with the squashing g left free, from the reduced action
   (arXiv:1010.0860); equal to the tensor route on generic jets to 3e-14.
2. Near-horizon throat: reproduces KKR at7–at9 (their at9 J = J1+J2). Perturbation
   exponents → non-integer gamma(y) (see concept page).
3. **Dead end:** gauge g = r² with the double zero factored out of b, f. Collocation
   becomes singular with N — near-extremal neighbours (b, f ~ 1/x) are almost
   representable by polynomials.
4. **KKR gauge** (b·g^rr fixed): two zero modes at fixed (a, alpha) — physical size and
   residual radial gauge — found as smooth, N-independent null vectors; fixed by
   g_H = 1 and no 1/r in P2. Replacing a row by the Hamiltonian constraint was wrong.
5. Coordinate s = r/(1+r) (the residual gauge is r³: half-integer in r²/(1+r²)).
   Unknowns P = 1 + (1−s)² Q, jets by product rule; mass is the value Q1(1)/2.
   Residuals generated in three expanded forms (cancellation near s = 1).
6. Practicalities: spurious aliasing roots (constraint ~1e-3) — rejected by the
   constraint and the Chebyshev tail; grid-scale noise at the round-off floor —
   filtered restart; at alpha ≳ 1 a tangent predictor is needed; accuracy is limited by
   a residual round-off floor that grows with N (best N ≈ 64–96 for 1e-7 in mu).

## Result

- y and mu at the manuscript's 12 couplings agree with its T → 0 extrapolation to
  ≤ 3.4e-7 and ≤ 6.2e-6; the slope (implicit differentiation at T = 0) agrees with its
  Psi_ext to ≤ 1.9e-5 — at most 1.8e-3 of the manuscript's 20-fit envelope. The
  extrapolated central values are far better than the envelope says.
- Slope: pi → minimum 1.071 at y ≈ 0.195 → rising (2.18 at y = 2.44); mu grows by 203 % over the branch.
- First law at T = 0 and Smarr at T = 0 hold along the branch (see report).
- KKR's extremal curves in fig. 1 reproduced to ~1e-3 (digitisation level) on x ≤ 0.863
  (alpha ≤ 3.118, r_H = 1); their range reaches x ≈ 0.92. Beyond x ≈ 0.863 the
  constraint violation next to the horizon exceeds 1e-4 even at N = 208/312 and the
  round-off floor forbids higher N: needs a horizon-stretching coordinate or extended
  precision, not more continuation.
